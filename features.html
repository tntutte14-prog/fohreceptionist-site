Questions

Good to know

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays your number. Calls are forwarded to FOH AI, so guests don't notice a change.

What if a guest wants to talk to a person?

The call is passed to a number you pick, or a message is taken with the guest's details so someone can call back.

Do guests need to press buttons?

No. They just talk, the way they would with your host, and FOH AI follows along.

What does it say about my restaurant?

Only what you've told us: your hours, address, menu, and booking rules. If it doesn't know, it hands the call to your team instead of guessing.

Who sets it up?

We do. We load your details, match the tone to your place, and test it with you before it answers a real call.

Ready to stop missing calls?

We're starting with a small number of restaurants. Tell us about yours and we'll set up a short call.
