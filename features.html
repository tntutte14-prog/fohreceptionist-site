How FOH AI works

Answers every call

The phone gets picked up whether you're slammed on a Friday or closed for the night. Guests get an answer, not a ringtone.

Takes reservations

It collects the date, time, party size and name, repeats them back, and gives your team a clean request to confirm.

Answers guest questions

Hours, parking, menu and dietary questions are answered from the details you give us, the same way every time.

Passes calls to your team

When a guest needs a person, the call goes to the number you choose, or a message is left with every detail.

Records every call

Look back at what was asked and what was said, so you always know what went on while you were busy.

Sounds like your place

We set the greeting and tone to fit your restaurant, so it comes across as part of your team.

Questions

Good to know

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Your number stays your number. Calls are forwarded to FOH AI, so guests don't notice a change.

What if a guest wants to talk to a person?

The call is passed to a number you pick, or a message is taken with the guest's details so someone can call back.

Do guests need to press buttons?

No. They just talk, the way they would with your host, and FOH AI follows along.

What does it say about my restaurant?

Only what you've told us: your hours, address, menu, and booking rules. If it doesn't know, it hands the call to your team instead of guessing.

Who sets it up?

We do. We load your details, match the tone to your place, and test it with you before it answers a real call.
