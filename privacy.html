Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

FOH AI is based in Langley, British Columbia, Canada. This policy covers this website and the emails you send us.

What we collect

  • This website has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics, and it does not set cookies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as part of running the service.
  • If you email us, we keep your name, email address and message so we can reply.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you and to run our business. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to host this website and our email. They handle information only to provide those services.

Restaurant customers

Information handled while FOH AI answers calls for a restaurant is covered by our agreement with that restaurant.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep emails for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep our business records. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing support@fohreceptionist.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.
