Questions

Good to know

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Guests call the number they already know. Nothing to reprint.

What if it gets asked something it can't answer?

It doesn't guess. If it can't answer something safely, it passes the call to your staff.

What happens when we're slammed?

It takes several calls at the same time, so a rush doesn't mean missed calls.

Can we make changes whenever we want?

Yes. You get your own login to view and change your settings any time. If you want something custom, tell us and we'll build it.

Does it take takeout and delivery orders?

Not yet. Takeout and delivery are coming soon. Right now it handles reservations, hours, the menu and most guest questions.

How fast can we start?

Onboarding is easy. We set up your hours, menu and rules, and you choose a voice.
