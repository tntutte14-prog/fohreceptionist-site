Why restaurants use it

The phone rings most when your team is busiest. FOH AI takes those calls so the guests in front of you keep your full attention.

Answer every call

Busy nights, lunch rushes and after close. Every call gets picked up, so guests don't hang up and try somewhere else.

Book more tables

Each call is a chance to take a reservation or bring a regular back. A missed call is often a lost booking.

Keep staff on the floor

Your team stays with the guests in the room. When a call needs a person, it goes to them with the details.
