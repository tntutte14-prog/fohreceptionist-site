Why we built it

A restaurant phone rings the most when the team is at its busiest. Calls go unanswered, and a missed call is often a missed booking. FOH AI answers those calls, so your team doesn't have to choose between the guest at the table and the phone.

How we work

We set up each restaurant ourselves, using your hours, menu and rules. We stay in touch while it runs and adjust it as your restaurant changes.
